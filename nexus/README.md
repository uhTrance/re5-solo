## Changelog

1.1.1

- Lost in Nightmares: one press at the piano plays it (it gave Chris's "never took piano lessons" line instead).

1.1.0

- Lost in Nightmares can be finished solo as Jill: examine spots and the piano fire on your press, the basement
  wake-up plays, the ceiling-trap cranks lock and the ceilings drop on the axe men, and the final fight saves its
  checkpoint.
- The launcher updates itself when a newer release is out.
- Fixes: 4-2 crashes on area change and quitting to the menu; fewer 120 fps catches on stairs; better crash logs.
